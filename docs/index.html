How it works

Everyone gets what they need.

Customers

  • Browse five service types
  • See the full price before ordering
  • Pay cash or card
  • Track, cancel and rate

Stores and sellers

  • Set up a store in a minute
  • Manage products and prices
  • Accept and prepare orders
  • Dropship items you don't stock

Drivers

  • Go online when you're free
  • Take jobs near you
  • Only one driver gets each job
  • Keep 80% of delivery fees and tips
